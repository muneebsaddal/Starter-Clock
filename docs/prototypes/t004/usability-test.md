# T004 moderated usability protocol

**Status:** Participant sessions required

**Prototype:** `index.html`

## Participants

Recruit five adults who maintain or have recently maintained a sourdough
starter: at least two newer bakers and two regular bakers. Do not recruit
project contributors. Record experience segment only; do not collect names.

## Setup

Use a 320–390 px mobile viewport with the Ready scenario and light or dark
appearance matching participant preference. Give one untimed practice attempt
to open and dismiss feeding entry. Reset before each measured task. Do not
explain interface labels or peak-window meaning.

## Tasks and pass criteria

1. Say: “You just fed Mabel with 25 g starter, 50 g flour, and 50 g water.
   Record that feeding.” Start timing when the task ends; stop when Save is
   activated. Pass at ≤15.0 seconds, saved successfully, without help.
2. Show the populated dashboard for ten seconds. Ask: “When might Mabel peak,
   and how certain is that?” Pass if the participant states a range equivalent
   to 4:30–6:00 PM and says it is approximate/estimated or may vary.
3. Ask the participant to find why the window was chosen, then switch to the
   Missing optional inputs scenario. Pass if they can name a recorded factor
   and understand why the second range is wider.
4. Show permission-denied and storage-failure scenarios. Pass if the
   participant can identify a next action and understands that feeding data is
   still loggable/preserved.
5. Show the Free boundary. Ask what is paid and whether old data is deleted.
   Record comprehension and perceived fairness; this is learning evidence, not
   authority to change scope or price.
